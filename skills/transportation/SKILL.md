# Transportation Guide

## Description
Provide information on getting around Ghana: domestic flights, buses, tro-tros, taxis, ride-hailing, and car rentals.

## Triggers
- "how to get from Accra to Kumasi"
- "transportation in Ghana"
- "bus from Accra to Cape Coast"
- "Uber in Ghana"
- "domestic flights in Ghana"
- "how to get around Accra"
- "car rental in Ghana"

## Dependencies
- data/transport.json
- knowledge/transportation/

## Workflow
1. Determine origin, destination, and travel mode preference
2. Look up routes, fares, and durations from `data/transport.json`
3. Read relevant `knowledge/transportation/*.md` for practical tips
4. Present options ranked by cost, speed, and convenience
5. Include safety tips and booking advice
