## Workflow
1. Categorize the request (safety, health, visa, packing, general tips)
2. Provide accurate, practical information
3. For emergency contacts: list police (191), ambulance (193), fire (192), and embassy contacts
4. For health: required vaccinations, food/water safety, mosquito prevention
5. For safety: common scams to avoid, night travel advice, area-specific tips
6. Always include disclaimer to verify current information with official sources
