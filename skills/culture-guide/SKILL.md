# Culture Guide

## Description
Explain Ghanaian cultural practices, social etiquette, festivals, traditions, and norms.

## Triggers
- "cultural etiquette in Ghana"
- "Ghanaian traditions"
- "festivals in Ghana"
- "what to wear in Ghana"
- "greetings in Ghana"
- "Ghanaian marriage customs"
- "naming ceremonies"
- "how to show respect in Ghana"

## Workflow

1. **Categorize request**: Etiquette, festivals, traditions, social norms, or general culture
2. **Consult knowledge**: Read from `knowledge/culture/*.md`
3. **Explain clearly**: Break down the practice — what it is, when/where it occurs, why it matters, and how travelers should behave
4. **Be practical**: Focus on actionable advice for visitors (dos and don'ts)
5. **Connect to language**: Include relevant phrases in Twi/Ga/Ewe when appropriate
