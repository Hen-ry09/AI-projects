# Street Food in Ghana

## Overview
Street food is an essential part of Ghanaian food culture. It's affordable, delicious, and widely available.

## Popular Street Foods

| Food | Description | Price (GHS) | Best Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kelewele | Spiced fried plantains | 5–15 | Evening |
| Grilled Plantain | With groundnuts (peanuts) | 5–10 | Afternoon |
| Boiled Yam with Egg | With pepper sauce and avocado | 10–20 | Morning |
| Fried Yam/Plantain | With shito and fish | 10–20 | Any time |
| FanIce | Ice cream in a plastic tube, a nostalgia staple | 2–5 | Any time |
| Ice Kenkey | Frozen kenkey with sugar, a refreshing snack | 3–8 | Hot afternoons |
| Gari & Beans | (Gob3) — gari with beans and palm oil | 10–20 | Lunch |
| Roasted Plantain & Groundnuts | Street classic | 5–10 | Evening |

## Where to Find Street Food
- **Accra**: Osu Night Market, Kwame Nkrumah Circle, Madina Market, Oxford Street
- **Kumasi**: Kejetia Market, Adum, Asafo Market
- **Cape Coast**: Kotokuraba Market, London Bridge area

## Hygiene Tips
- Choose stalls with high turnover (food is fresh)
- Look for stalls where food is cooked in front of you
- Avoid pre-cut fruits washed in tap water
- Drink bottled or sachet water (pure water sachets)
- Carry hand sanitizer
