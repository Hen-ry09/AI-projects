# Taxis & Ride-Hailing in Ghana

## Ride-Hailing Apps
| App | Coverage | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Uber** | Accra, Kumasi, Tema | Widely available in Accra |
| **Bolt** | Accra, Kumasi, Tema, Takoradi, Cape Coast | Often cheaper than Uber |
| **Yango** | Accra, Kumasi | Newer entrant, competitive pricing |

### Tips for Ride-Hailing
- Fares: Accra city rides 20–80 GHS; airport to Osu ~50–80 GHS
- Payment: Cash or mobile money (MoMo) — card not always accepted
- Surge pricing happens during rush hours (7–9 AM, 4–7 PM) and late nights
- Always confirm the license plate before getting in
- Drivers may call you — if you don't speak Twi, ask if they speak English

## Street Taxis
- Color-coded by region: Yellow (Accra), Yellow with orange stripe (Kumasi)
- **How to flag**: Stand on roadside and wave — drivers will honk to indicate availability
- **Negotiate first**: Agree on fare before getting in (or insist on meter — rare)
- **Short rides**: 15–30 GHS in Accra
- **Longer trips**: Negotiate — Accra to Tema ~80–120 GHS
- **"Drop"**: Shared taxi, you pay per seat — cheapest way (5–15 GHS per drop)

## Safety Tips
- Use ride-hailing apps at night for traceability
- Share your trip status with someone
- Avoid unmarked cars claiming to be taxis
- Most drivers are honest but agree on the fare upfront

## Car Rentals
- **Local agencies**: Airport, Osu, East Legon — ~200–500 GHS/day for a sedan
- **International**: Avis, Hertz available at the airport (more expensive)
- **Requirements**: Valid driver's license from your country or international driving permit
- **Driving**: Drive on the right. Road conditions vary widely. Night driving is risky outside cities

## Motorcycle Taxis (Okada)
- Illegal in Accra but widely used in smaller cities and rural areas
- Convenient for traffic but higher accident risk
- **Always negotiate first**: 5–20 GHS for short trips
- Helmet should be provided — insist on wearing one
