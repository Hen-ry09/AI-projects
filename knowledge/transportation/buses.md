# Buses & Tro-Tros — Getting Around Ghana

## Intercity Buses (VIP & Standard)

### Major Bus Companies
| Company | Routes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| STC (State Transport Company) | All major routes | Government-run, reliable, AC |
| VIP Transport | Accra ↔ Kumasi, Accra ↔ Takoradi, Accra ↔ Tamale | Very comfortable, AC, TVs |
| OA Travel & Transport | Accra ↔ Kumasi, Accra ↔ Sunyani | Good mid-range option |
| Metro Mass | All major routes | Budget option, no AC, stops frequently |
| Kufuor Transport | Accra ↔ Kumasi | Express service |

### Sample Fares (Approximate)
| Route | Distance | VIP Fare (GHS) | STC Fare (GHS) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accra → Kumasi | 260 km | 120–180 | 80–120 | 4–5 hrs |
| Accra → Cape Coast | 150 km | 60–90 | 40–60 | 2.5–3 hrs |
| Accra → Takoradi | 220 km | 100–150 | 70–100 | 3.5–4 hrs |
| Accra → Tamale | 650 km | 250–350 | 180–250 | 10–12 hrs |

### Tips
- VIP buses are worth the extra cost for long journeys
- Book VIP tickets 1–2 days ahead (especially on Fridays and holidays)
- Boarding points are often chaotic — arrive 30 min early
- Snacks and water are sold at rest stops

## Tro-Tro (Minibus)
- The most common and cheapest form of public transport
- Operate on fixed routes with set fares (hail them at designated stations)
- They wait until full before departing — patience required
- Fares: 5–20 GHS depending on distance (city routes)
- Terminal points are chaotic but organized by route

## Metro Mass
- Larger government buses, cheapest option
- No AC, often crowded, frequent stops
- Good for short to medium distances
- Fares: 10–50 GHS depending on distance
