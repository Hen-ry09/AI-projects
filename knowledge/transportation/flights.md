# Flights — Getting to & Around Ghana

## International Airports
- **Kotoka International Airport (ACC)** — Accra. Main entry point. Direct flights from London, New York, Dubai, Amsterdam, Addis Ababa, Nairobi, Brussels, Istanbul, Doha, and more
- **Kumasi International Airport (KMS)** — Newly upgraded. Limited international services
- **Tamale Airport (TML)** — Domestic flights; occasional international charters

## Major Airlines Serving Ghana
- **Delta** — New York–JFK to Accra
- **British Airways** — London Heathrow to Accra
- **United** — Washington Dulles to Accra
- **KLM** — Amsterdam to Accra
- **Emirates** — Dubai to Accra
- **Ethiopian Airlines** — Addis Ababa to Accra (excellent connections across Africa)
- **Qatar Airways** — Doha to Accra
- **Turkish Airlines** — Istanbul to Accra
- **Air France** — Paris CDG to Accra
- **Brussels Airlines** — Brussels to Accra
- **Africa World Airlines (AWA)** — Domestic flights: Accra ↔ Kumasi, Accra ↔ Tamale
- **PassionAir** — Domestic flights: Accra ↔ Kumasi, Accra ↔ Takoradi

## Domestic Tips
- AWA and PassionAir fly between Accra, Kumasi, Tamale, and Takoradi
- Flights are affordable (~500–1200 GHS one-way)
- Book at least 1–2 weeks in advance during peak season (Dec–Jan)
- Baggage allowance is usually 23 kg checked + 7 kg carry-on

## Visa & Entry
- Many nationalities can get a visa on arrival (check current policy)
- e-Visa available for many countries — apply online before travel
- Passport must be valid for at least 6 months
- Yellow fever vaccination certificate is required for entry
