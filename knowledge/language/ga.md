# Ga Language Guide

## Overview
Ga is the language of the Ga people, indigenous to the Greater Accra Region. It's spoken by ~750,000 people. Accra, the capital, sits on traditional Ga land.

## Pronunciation Guide
- **a** — like "a" in "father"
- **e** — like "e" in "bet"
- **ɛ** — like "e" in "bed"
- **i** — like "ee" in "see"
- **o** — like "o" in "go"
- **ɔ** — like "aw" in "law"
- **u** — like "oo" in "food"
- **gb** — single sound, like "gb" in "good boy" said quickly
- **kp** — single sound, like "kp" in "backpack" said quickly
- **ny** — like "ny" in "canyon"
- **ŋ** — like "ng" in "sing"
- Ga is a tonal language — tone distinguishes meaning
