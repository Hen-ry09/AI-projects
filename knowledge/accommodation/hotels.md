# Hotels & Lodging in Ghana

## Overview
Ghana offers a wide range of accommodation from budget guesthouses to luxury international hotels and eco-lodges.

## Accommodation Types

| Type | Description | Price Range (GHS/night) |
|---|---|---|
| Budget Guesthouse | Basic rooms, shared facilities, fan only | 100–300 |
| Mid-range Hotel | Private room, AC, WiFi, restaurant, pool possible | 400–1,500 |
| Luxury Hotel | International-standard, full amenities, spa | 1,500–5,000 |
| Eco-lodge | Nature-focused, sustainable, unique locations | 500–3,000 |
| Beach Resort | Coastal properties with beach access | 500–3,000 |
| Serviced Apartment | Self-catering, kitchen, longer stays | 300–1,500 |
| Hostel | Dormitory beds, budget travelers | 50–150 |

## By City

### Accra
- **Luxury**: Labadi Beach Hotel (beachfront, 1,500–3,000 GHS), Mövenpick Ambassador (central, 2,000–4,000 GHS), Kempinski Hotel Gold Coast (ultra-luxury, 2,500–5,000 GHS)
- **Mid-range**: African Regent Hotel (Airport Residential, 800–1,500 GHS), Accra City Hotel (former Novotel, central, 600–1,200 GHS)
- **Budget**: Somewhere Nice Guesthouse (Labone, 200–400 GHS), Rising Phoenix Hostel (Osu, 100–250 GHS)

### Kumasi
- **Luxury**: Golden Tulip Kumasi (1,000–2,000 GHS), Kumasi City Hotel (800–1,500 GHS)
- **Mid-range**: Hotel DuBois City Center (400–800 GHS), Noda Hotel (300–600 GHS)
- **Budget**: Phil Lodge (150–300 GHS)

### Cape Coast
- **Mid-range**: Ridge Royal Hotel (400–800 GHS), Elmina Bay Resort (500–1,000 GHS)
- **Budget**: Sam's Lodge (150–300 GHS), Beachside Lodge (200–400 GHS)

### Mole National Park
- **Luxury**: Zaina Lodge (2,000–5,000 GHS, game viewing deck)
- **Budget**: Mole Motel (250–500 GHS, basic but inside park)
