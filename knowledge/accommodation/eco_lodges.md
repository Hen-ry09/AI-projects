# Eco-Lodges & Unique Stays in Ghana

## Overview
Ghana has a growing eco-lodge scene, especially around national parks, beaches, and the Volta Region.

## Notable Eco-Lodges

| Lodge | Location | Features | Price (GHS/night) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zaina Lodge | Mole National Park | Solar-powered, game deck, luxury tents | 2,000–5,000 |
| Tafi Atome Homestay | Volta Region | Monkey sanctuary village, community-run | 100–250 |
| Big Milly's Backyard | Kokrobite Beach | Beachfront bungalows, reggae vibe | 200–500 |
| Lou Moon Resort | Busua Beach | Surfers' spot, beachfront cabins | 300–800 |
| Watertight Resort | Shama (Western) | Overwater bungalows, quiet beach | 400–1,000 |

## Tips
- Book eco-lodges well in advance, especially during peak season (Dec–Mar)
- Many eco-lodges are off-grid — expect limited electricity and no WiFi
- Community-run lodges offer authentic cultural experiences
- Bring insect repellent and a flashlight for remote locations
