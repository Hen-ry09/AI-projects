# Cape Coast — History & Heritage

## Overview
Cape Coast is a historic city in the Central Region, famous for its role in the trans-Atlantic slave trade and its well-preserved colonial architecture.

## Key Attractions

| Attraction | Description | Approx. Entry Fee (GHS) |
|---|---|---|
| Cape Coast Castle | UNESCO World Heritage site; former slave trading post with museum | 40–65 |
| Kakum National Park | Rainforest with canopy walkway 40m above ground | 60–80 (citizens), 150–200 (non-citizens) |
| Fort Victoria & Fort William | Colonial forts with panoramic views of the city | 10–20 |
| Cape Coast Centre for National Culture | Arts and crafts market | Free |
| Kotokuraba Market | Local market with fresh produce and traditional goods | Free |
| Brenu Beach | Quiet beach resort area just outside town | Free–20 |

## Canopy Walkway (Kakum)
- 350m long suspended bridge walkway, 40m above forest floor
- Guided tours available (1–2 hours)
- Best visited early morning (less crowded, more wildlife)
- Wear comfortable shoes and bring insect repellent

## Tips
- Cape Coast is ~2.5–3 hours drive from Accra
- Combine with Elmina (20 min away) for a full heritage day trip
- Guided tours at the castles are highly recommended
- Accommodation: mid-range lodges and beach resorts available
- Avoid swimming at beaches near the castle (strong currents)
