# Kakum National Park

## Overview
Kakum National Park covers 375 km² of pristine tropical rainforest in Ghana's Central Region. Its main draw is the famous Canopy Walkway, a series of suspension bridges 40 meters above the forest floor.

## Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Canopy Walkway | 350m long, 7 bridges, 40m high |
| Wildlife | Forest elephants, bongo antelope, 600+ butterfly species, 250+ bird species |
| Trails | Guided nature walks (1–3 hours) |
| Operating Hours | 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily |

## Activities
- **Canopy Walk** — The signature experience (allow 30–45 min)
- **Guided Forest Walk** — Spot wildlife and learn about medicinal plants
- **Night Walk** — See nocturnal animals (by arrangement)
- **Birdwatching** — Over 250 species including the yellow-headed picathartes

## Practical Info
- **Location**: 33 km north of Cape Coast, ~3 hours from Accra
- **Entry**: Citizens 60 GHS, non-citizens 150–200 GHS (walkway included)
- **Guide fee**: ~50 GHS for a guided forest walk
- **Best time**: Early morning (6 AM opening) for wildlife and cooler weather
- **What to bring**: Long pants, closed shoes, insect repellent, rain jacket, water

## Tips
- The canopy walkway is safe but may sway — not recommended if you have severe vertigo
- Rainy season (May–July) means slippery walkways and more mosquitoes
- Combine with a visit to Cape Coast Castle for a full day trip from Accra
