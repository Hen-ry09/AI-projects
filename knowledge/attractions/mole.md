# Mole National Park

## Overview
Mole National Park is Ghana's largest and most prestigious wildlife reserve, spanning 4,840 km² in the Savannah Region. It's the best place in Ghana to see elephants, antelopes, baboons, and over 300 bird species.

## Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Size | 4,840 km² |
| Key Wildlife | Elephants, buffalo, kob, roan antelope, warthog, baboon, monkey, 300+ bird species |
| Rare Sightings | Lion, leopard, hyena (elusive) |
| Operating Hours | 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily |

## Activities
- **Game Drives** — Morning (6 AM) and afternoon (3 PM) guided drives in park vehicles
- **Walking Safaris** — Guided walks with armed ranger (highly recommended)
- **Birdwatching** — Over 300 species including Abyssinian roller, white-crowned shrike
- **Community Walks** — Visit nearby villages (by arrangement)

## Practical Info
- **Location**: Savannah Region, near Larabanga. ~12 hours drive from Accra, or fly to Tamale (1 hr) then 2 hr drive
- **Entry**: Citizens ~30 GHS, non-citizens ~100–150 GHS (per day)
- **Game drive**: ~100–200 GHS per vehicle (shared among group)
- **Walking safari**: ~50–100 GHS per person
- **Accommodation**: Mole Motel (park-run, basic) or Zaina Lodge (luxury, near park)
- **Best season**: December–April (dry season, animals gather at water holes)

## Getting There
1. **Drive** from Accra: ~12 hours via Kumasi–Techiman–Kintampo route
2. **Fly** to Tamale (1 hr from Accra), then 2-hour drive to park
3. **Bus** from Accra to Tamale (STC or VIP buses, ~10 hours), then shared taxi to park

## Tips
- Book game drives at the reception desk upon arrival
- Bring binoculars, sunscreen, hat, and plenty of water
- The park has virtually no phone signal — download offline maps
- Dry season (Dec–Apr) is best for elephant sightings near water holes
- Wet season (May–Oct): lush scenery, fewer visitors, harder to spot wildlife
