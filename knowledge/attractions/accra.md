# Accra — Ghana's Capital

## Overview
Accra is the vibrant capital of Ghana, a coastal city blending modern development with rich history, lively markets, and a thriving arts and food scene.

## Key Attractions

| Attraction | Description | Approx. Entry Fee (GHS) |
|---|---|---|
| Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park | Mausoleum and museum dedicated to Ghana's first president | 40–60 |
| Independence Square | Historic square with the Independence Arch and Black Star Gate | Free |
| Makola Market | Bustling traditional market — great for fabrics, spices, crafts | Free (bring cash) |
| Osu Castle (Christiansborg Castle) | Historic Danish-built castle, seat of government | 20–40 |
| Jamestown | Historic fishing district with lighthouse and colonial architecture | 20 (lighthouse) |
| Labadi Beach | Popular public beach with music, food, and horse rides | 10–20 |
| W.E.B. Du Bois Center | Museum and final resting place of the civil rights activist | 20–30 |
| Artists Alliance Gallery | Contemporary African art gallery in Osu | Free |
| National Museum of Ghana | Museum covering Ghana's cultural heritage and history | 10–20 |
| Aburi Botanical Gardens | 30-min drive from Accra; beautiful gardens and cool climate | 30–50 |

## Neighborhoods
- **Osu** — Nightlife, restaurants, shopping (Oxford Street)
- **Airport Residential** — Upscale hotels, embassies, quiet streets
- **East Legon** — Trendy restaurants, lounges, residential area
- **Jamestown** — Historic, artsy, great for walking tours
- **Cantonments** — Diplomatic area, peaceful, green

## Tips
- Accra traffic is heavy — plan 2× your expected travel time
- Uber and Bolt are widely available and reliable
- Trotro (minibus) routes connect all parts of the city for very low fares
- Most attractions accept mobile money (MoMo) or cash
- Best dry season visiting: November–March
